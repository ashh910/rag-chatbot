Solstice Robotics Announces GROVE-2 Agricultural Robot,
Cuts Herbicide Use by 78% in Field Trials

AUSTIN, TX — July 14, 2026 — Solstice Robotics Inc. today unveiled the
GROVE-2, the second generation of its autonomous weeding and harvesting
robot for row-crop farms. In year-long field trials across twelve farms
in California's Central Valley, the GROVE-2 reduced herbicide
application by 78% compared to conventional spraying methods while
maintaining crop yield within 2% of baseline.

"GROVE-2 represents three years of work on our vision-guided precision
actuation system," said Ines Okafor, Chief Technology Officer of
Solstice Robotics. "Farmers get the same results with a fraction of
the chemical inputs, and the robot pays for itself in under two
growing seasons."

The GROVE-2 uses a twelve-camera stereo-vision array paired with a
custom edge-inference chip to identify and mechanically remove weeds
at a rate of up to 1.4 acres per hour. The unit is fully solar-assisted
and can operate for up to 14 hours between charges.

Pricing for the GROVE-2 starts at $84,500 per unit, with a
subscription-based "Robotics-as-a-Service" leasing option available
starting at $2,100/month. General availability is planned for
September 2026, with pre-orders opening August 1.
